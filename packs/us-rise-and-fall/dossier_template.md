# Story dossier template

One dossier per video. It is the permanent record of the topic: everything
found, everything used, everything cut, and what was learned after release.

## 1. Discoveries
Notable facts found during research, grouped by theme (for example: origins,
the build, operations, money, people, disasters, decline, today). Each fact
carries a source link. Anything not yet confirmed by a second source is marked
"(verify)".

## 2. Key numbers
Money, sizes, counts, durations and dates that matter, each with a source and,
for money, a modern equivalent.

## 3. Cast
Every person involved: name, role, years, one human detail, and why they
matter to the story.

## 4. Timeline
Dated events in order, from the earliest background to the present day.

## 5. Quotes on record
Exact words from the time (newspapers, reports, letters, speeches,
interviews), each with speaker or publication, date and link. Quotes are
copied exactly as published.

## 6. Source shelf
Articles, books, maps, archives, films and pages worth keeping.

## 7. Visual plan
Ideas for what the viewer sees: footage searches, maps, before-and-after
moments, animated numbers, quote cards.

## 8. Credits
Footage, images and music used, with source and licence, for the video
description.

## 9. Open questions
Research gaps and angles still worth checking.

## 10. Must-include
Points and lines the script has to cover.

## 11. Cutting room
Script passages and facts removed from the final cut but worth keeping.

## 12. Thumbnail
The chosen thumbnail and the alternatives.

## 13. Script
The final narration script.

## 14. Post-release notes
After publishing: views, retention, comments worth noting, and what to do
differently next time.
